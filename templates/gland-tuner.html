◆ Category thresholds — rows are peak-period bands, columns are categories
Rules are matched top to bottom and the first hit wins, so order is priority — put the most specific rule first. Ranges are [min, max]: min inclusive, max exclusive, inf for unbounded. Leave direction blank to ignore it; a rule with a direction can only ever match when the swell direction is known.
◆ Live preview — height (ft) × period (s)
Pick a brush above and click or drag across the matrix to paint ratings straight onto it. Painted cells become high-priority rules on save — adjacent cells of the same rating are merged into the largest possible rectangles, so the rule list stays readable. Erase removes a painted cell and lets the rules below show through.
