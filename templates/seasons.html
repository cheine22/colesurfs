Fun+ swell is primary swell tall enough for its period to rate FUN or better: —. Surfable wind is sustained wind that is —. A fun+ day has at least two daylight 3-hour windows of fun+ swell in which at least one spot in the region also had surfable wind; the day takes the highest rating that two such windows reached. Swell is observed at the region's NDBC buoy; wind comes from the EURO (ECMWF) model. A drought is the run of non-fun+ days between one fun+ swell and the next; each season also counts the run from its first day to its first fun+ swell and from its last fun+ swell to its end (today, while the season is in progress), so a drought that straddles two seasons is counted in both.
